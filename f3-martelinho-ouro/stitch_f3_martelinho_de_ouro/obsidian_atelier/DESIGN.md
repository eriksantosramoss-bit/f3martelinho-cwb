## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the pinnacle of precision automotive restoration, bespoke paint correction, and artistic dent craft. It translates the surgical perfection of a high-end inspection studio—where linear LED light tunnels reveal the slightest surface imperfection—into a digital experience of high contrast, dark luxury, and mechanical elegance.

### Emotional Response & Target Audience
- **Target Audience:** Discerning luxury automobile owners, supercar collectors, sports car enthusiasts, and perfectionists who view their vehicles as fine art.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Uncompromising prestige, craftsmanship, surgical authority, and peace of mind. Every screen evokes the feel of entering a state-of-the-art detailing bay at night under focused floodlights.

### Visual Style
A hybrid of **Ultra-Dark Luxury Minimalist** and **Reflective Studio Glassmorphism**. Deep obsidian and graphite layers set a mood of quiet power, while metallic gold accents and specular icy-white highlights echo the polished curvature of coated bodywork. Interfaces feature razor-sharp linear geometry, subtle amber edge glints, and luminous WhatsApp conversion points designed for rapid, VIP-tier client intake.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around dark room automotive inspection lighting. The canvas stays anchored in deep obsidian, enabling gold trims and specular highlights to guide gaze movement directly toward transformation showcases and direct consultation triggers.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Gold (`#D4AF37`):** The signature gold foil tone representing the mastery of the "Martelinho de Ouro". Reserved for primary call-to-actions, active indicators, brand monograms, and key conversion badges.
- **Secondary Amber Glow (`#F59E0B`):** A high-vibrancy metallic tone used for linear gradients, glowing borders, hover states, and service milestone metrics.
- **Tertiary VIP WhatsApp Green (`#22C55E`):** A vibrant emerald tone dedicated strictly to instant consultation, quote requests via WhatsApp, and live availability statuses.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#0B0E14`):** The foundational obsidian dark void. Layered upward through deep graphite (`#161B22`) for mid-ground containers and charcoal (`#1F242D`) for elevated interactive cards.
- **Specular Studio Accents (`#FFFFFF`, `#E2E8F0`, `#94A3B8`):** High-contrast studio light whites that provide surgical legibility, razor-thin card strokes, and crisp metallic typography.

## Typography

The type system blends the aerodynamic, sculptural curves of **Outfit** for structural headings with the clinical, uncompromised legibility of **Inter** for technical body copy and specifications.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Headlines (Outfit):** Set with tight tracking to mirror the sculpted lines of sports vehicle panels. Uppercase treatments with wide letter-spacing (`label-caps`) are used for service categories, car segment classifications, and status indicators.
- **Body & Specs (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral, and balanced. Used for repair process descriptions, warranty terms, and before/after technical logs.
- **Specials:** Display headlines often feature linear gradient fills shifting from studio white (`#FFFFFF`) to icy metallic silver (`#94A3B8`) or from primary gold (`#D4AF37`) to amber (`#F59E0B`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on a 12-column fluid grid system on desktop (max width 1320px) and a single-column 4-grid module on mobile, creating structured alignment reminiscent of technical workshop blueprints.

### Spacing Philosophy
- **Negative Space:** Generous vertical sections give each automotive project and bespoke service the aura of an exclusive showroom exhibit.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an 8px grid baseline (`space-xs` through `space-xl`).
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12 columns, 24px gutters, generous 48px outer margins.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px outer margins.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 20px margins; content reflows into vertical service stacks with sticky WhatsApp booking docks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on traditional muddy shadows; instead, it uses car-showroom lighting: glossy translucent overlays, fine metallic specular borders, and focused ambient glows.

### Tonal Hierarchy
- **Base Surface (`#0B0E14`):** Canvas background. Absorbs ambient light like deep obsidian paint.
- **Mid Surface (`#161B22`):** Structural sidebars, content grouping panels, and modal shells.
- **Elevated Interactive Surface (`#1F242D`):** Feature cards and interactive modules, surfaced with a 1px top border gradient (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` to `transparent`) resembling horizontal studio light tubes reflecting across a bonnet.

### Glassmorphism & Studio Lighting
- **Cards & Overlays:** `rgba(22, 27, 34, 0.75)` with `16px` backdrop-blur and a subtle `1px` border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Glow Emitters:** Critical CTAs and golden badges cast a soft, diffused radial glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 32px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.22)`.
- **WhatsApp Accent Glow:** High-priority contact surfaces use `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.28)`.

## Shapes

The form language echoes modern luxury automotive engineering: precision-milled corners, aerodynamic flow, and tight structural tolerances. 

### Corner Radius System
- **Base (`0.5rem / 8px`):** Used for input fields, micro-badges, and technical tags to maintain precision.
- **Medium (`1rem / 16px`):** Applied to cards, transformation image frames, and service containers, mimicking curved aerodynamic spoilers and windshield contours.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for hero action buttons, floating WhatsApp triggers, and status pills to create ergonomic touchpoints.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Prestige (Gold):** Background filled with linear gradient (135deg, `#D4AF37` to `#F59E0B`), text in rich `#0B0E14` bold Outfit, full pill shape. On hover: intense ambient gold bloom and 1px inward specular edge.
- **Direct VIP WhatsApp Button:** High-visibility green `#22C55E`, glowing drop shadow, bold white typography, accompanied by the WhatsApp mark. Fixed floating trigger on mobile screens.
- **Secondary Ghost:** Transparent body, 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`, white text. On hover: border transitions to `#D4AF37` with subtle obsidian glow.

### Cards & Service Tiers
- Built using glassmorphic charcoal layers (`#161B22` at 85% opacity with 12px blur).
- Bordered with an asymmetric highlight: 1px top and left border in light gold/white reflection, falling off into dark graphite at the bottom.
- Hover states include a vertical micro-lift (-4px) and an outer gold aura.

### Transformation Comparison Slider (Before & After)
- Custom dual-image split screen showing damaged panel vs. restored mirror finish.
- Divided by a razor-thin 2px golden vertical line capped with a round, milled-metal drag handle containing dual gold chevrons.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: deep `#0B0E14` with a 1px border in `#1F242D`.
- Focus State: border shifts to `#D4AF37` with an ambient amber focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`).
- Input labels rendered in `label-caps` typography in `#94A3B8`.

### Badges & Micro-tags
- Compact pill-shaped containers with 1px border in `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`, subtle dark gold background tint (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`), and crisp uppercase Outfit typography. Used for car classes ("Supercar Certified", "Ceramic Pro", "Original Paint Kept").
